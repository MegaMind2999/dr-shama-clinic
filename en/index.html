What he sees

From blur to focus

What short sight does to the world, and how treatment can change it.

Glasses on: the far shore is sharp.

With short sight, light from far away comes to a focus in front of the retina. His glasses bend it just enough to land on the retina itself.

Glasses off: the distance blurs.

Lamps along the Corniche turn into soft discs and the citadel loses its edges, while everything close by stays clear.

Treatment can move the focus back.

Laser correction reshapes the cornea. An implantable lens adds a correction inside the eye. Either way the focus returns to the retina, which can reduce the need for glasses.

Your examination decides.

Glasses, contact lenses, laser or a lens implant: which suits you depends on your eyes, and only an examination can show it.

From your first call to your visit

There’s no online booking system. Appointments are arranged with the clinic by phone.

  1. 1

    Call the clinic

    Call 03 4230484 and tell the team what’s troubling your eyes, or which treatment you’d like to ask about.

  2. 2

    Ask which consultation you need

    The clinic can tell you which kind of appointment fits your question and what it involves.

  3. 3

    Confirm your appointment

    Agree a date and time with the clinic. Your appointment is booked once the clinic confirms it.

  4. 4

    Visit with your records

    Bring your glasses or contact lenses, previous eye reports or scans, and a list of the medicines you take.

Some examinations use drops that widen the pupil and blur vision for a few hours. Ask when you book, and arrange not to drive afterwards.

All questions
How do I book an appointment?

Call the clinic on 03 4230484 or 03 4230196, or on its mobile number, 0122 318 8320. You can also message the same mobile number on WhatsApp. Appointments are arranged and confirmed by phone.

Where is the clinic?

In El Bassant Tower (Azhar El Saraya Towers) on Fawzy Moaz Street at the corner of Albert El Awal Street, Smouha, Alexandria, above the HSBC Smouha branch.

When is the clinic open?

Clinic hours aren’t published online yet. Please call 03 4230484 to confirm a time before you visit.

What should I bring?

Your current glasses or contact lenses, any previous eye reports, prescriptions or scans, and a list of the medicines and eye drops you use. If you might be given drops that widen the pupil, bring sunglasses and someone to accompany you.

Can I have laser vision correction?

That depends on your cornea and prescription, which only an examination can show. For some people with short-sight or astigmatism who aren’t suited to laser correction, an EVO ICL implantable lens may be an option.
